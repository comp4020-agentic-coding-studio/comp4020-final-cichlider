Turf War

You're a paint roller. Wherever you roll turns your team's colour. Paint nobody rolls over again fades away, so keep your turf fresh, and take theirs.

  • Drive: W/S or ↑/↓ forward and back, A/D or ←/→ to turn. On a phone, use the buttons.
  • Bots 🤖 fill in when there aren't enough people, and step out when someone joins.
  • Colours: pick a pair at the bottom. It only changes your screen.
