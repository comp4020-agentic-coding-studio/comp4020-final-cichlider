    The Wall

    One very large wall that anyone can paint on. Whoever else is here is painting right now — you'll see their cursors.

    • Paint: drag. Tools on the left, colours below.
    • Move: hold Space and drag, use ✋, two fingers, or scroll. Ctrl+scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Wander: 🔥 jumps to where people are painting, ⬚ to a fresh patch.
    • Too busy? Hide any painter from your own view in the panel on the right.
