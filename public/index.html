Turf War

You're a paint roller on a wall. Everywhere you roll turns your team's colour, and wet paint runs down. Paint nobody rolls over again fades away, so keep your turf fresh, and take theirs.

  • Roll: W S forward and back, A D to turn (or the arrow keys). On a phone, use the buttons.
  • Bots fill in when there aren't enough people, and step out when someone joins.
  • Colours: pick a pair in the corner. It only changes your screen.
