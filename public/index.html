Turf War

Two teams, one floor. When the clock runs out, the team whose colour covers more of it wins.

  • Roll: drag across the floor. Your roller paints your team's colour, over anything.
  • Ink: rolling drains your tank. Lift off and it refills.
  • Look around: hold Space and drag, two fingers, or scroll. Ctrl+scroll or pinch to zoom.
