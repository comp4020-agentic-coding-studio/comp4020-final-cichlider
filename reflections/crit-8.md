# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The idea wasn't new this week. I first wanted a shared wall people could draw
on while I was working on my personal website, but it stayed an idea: a
personal site is one person's space, and a wall only works when other people
are on it. The final project brief asked for exactly the thing that was
missing (multi-user, real-time, persistent), so the breakthrough was realising
I already had the answer to "what's worth building". With the concept settled,
the week went into the decisions that make it good: anyone can paint, nobody
can erase anyone else, and you can hide whoever you don't want to see, but only
in your own view.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made it clear that I want to be a developer who cares about interaction.
What makes the wall worth opening isn't the server or the database. It's how
the brush feels, seeing someone else's cursor move next to yours, and being
able to wander off to an empty patch. Those details are what I want to spend my
time getting right.
